# KiasuHealth Demo Prompts 🏆🇸🇬

Copy and paste these prompts into Telegram during the demo.

---

### 1. The "Gossip" Check (Social Intelligence)
**Context**: Checking on the competition to stay motivated.
**Prompt**:
```text
/ask how are my kakis doing?
```

---

### 2. Meal Logging (Vision AI)
**Context**: Upload a meal photo (e.g., Mentaiko Salmon) with this caption to get a balance score.
**Caption**:
```text
/photo
```

---

### 3. The "Escape Route" (Personalized Nudge)
**Context**: Finding a specific way to overtake the current leader (Chris).
**Prompt**:
```text
/ask where can I walk to beat Chris?
```

---

### 4. High-Stakes Mission (Location-based)
**Context**: Checking for nearby activities while near Marina Bay to avoid the "Kopi Tax".
**Prompt**:
```text
/ask are there any parks near Marina Bay?
```

---

### 5. The Sunday Reveal (League Standings)
**Context**: Run this in the **Group Chat** to announce the weekly winner and loser.
**Prompt**:
```text
/reveal
```

---

### 6. General AI Coaching (Custom Query)
**Context**: Any other health-related question in private DM.
**Example**:
```text
/ask should I eat laksa or fish soup for dinner to keep my lead?
```
