## My recommendation, given your profile

Top 2 for you:

1. **Unified clinical education (NUH)**  
   - You can build:  
     - An Elastic‑backed **education ops index** (rotations, slots, trainees, supervisors).  
     - A **planner assistant** agent that, given a trainee and date range, suggests rotations based on rules and historical patterns.  
     - Search + dashboards for capacity, conflicts, and bottlenecks.  
   - This matches Elastic’s preferred stories (healthcare intelligence, RAG on complex docs, scheduling/ops from search). [elastic](https://www.elastic.co/blog/takeaways-forge-the-future-hackathon)

2. **United recruitment system (MCI)**  
   - You can build:  
     - Candidate + assignment index in Elasticsearch.  
     - A **recruiter copilot** that finds best matches, surfaces risks (no‑shows, low retention), and answers policy questions.  
     - Visualisations of funnel, fill rates, retention per role.  
   - Close to your fintech/people‑data interests and exactly the kind of “real-time intelligence” Elastic pushes. [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/elastic-real-world-5-uses-youll-actually-see-2025-4euec)

If you want something more “sexy” visually and don’t mind stretching: facade monitoring as an event‑analytics + safety‑copilot platform on top of CV events is viable, but it’s a bit further from your usual playground. [cdata](https://www.cdata.com/blog/elasticsearch-use-cases)
