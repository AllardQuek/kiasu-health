Here’s the short version of the Elastic Forge The Future Singapore hackathon details. [elastic-forge-the-future.devpost](https://elastic-forge-the-future.devpost.com)

## Basics

- **Name**: Elastic Forge The Future Singapore. [devpost](https://devpost.com/software/elastic-forge-the-future)
- **Date & time**: 13 March 2026, 10am–3pm (about 5 hours). [eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/elastic-forge-the-future-singapore-tickets-1982424673564)

## What you’re supposed to build

- Build a **real‑world use case** using Elastic (Search / Observability / Security) + AI, not just slideware. [elastic](https://www.elastic.co/blog/takeaways-forge-the-future-hackathon)
- Turn **data + AI into “real‑world intelligence”** for a specific industry (banking, manufacturing, healthcare, ecommerce, public sector, etc.). [siliconangle](https://siliconangle.com/2024/12/05/elastic-pioneering-future-search-ai-cloudhyperscaleraws/)
- Expect to show something actually working: ingest some data, query it, and have an AI/agent or analytics layer doing something non‑trivial.

## Judging & criteria

Judges include senior people from Elastic, AI Singapore, AWS, and CSIT. [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/laurenceliew_happy-to-be-back-judgingelastics-forge-activity-7418902795405410304-vku6)

- **Technical implementation** – 30% (Bonus for Elastic Agent Builder)
